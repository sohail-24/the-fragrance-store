# Layout Principles

- Mobile-first design with a simple, touch-friendly interface.
- Sticky header keeps Back and Share actions easily accessible.
- Product image and gallery are displayed first for quick visual identification.
- Essential product information (Name, Grade, Price, Rating, Stock, MOQ) appears immediately below the image.
- Pricing, stock status, and MOQ remain visible before purchase actions.
- Quantity selector is optimized for thumb-friendly interaction with large tap targets.
- Live total updates instantly as the quantity changes.
- Primary purchase actions are stacked vertically for easy one-handed use:
  - Add & Continue Shopping
  - Add to Cart & Go to Cart
  - Buy Now
- Product Description, Specifications, and Supplier Information are presented as separate sections for better readability.
- Related Products appear at the bottom to encourage continued browsing after reviewing the current product.
- Content follows a natural top-to-bottom purchasing flow with minimal scrolling.
- Consistent spacing, typography, and component sizing improve readability on small screens.
- The interface maintains visual consistency with the FreshFlow Home Marketplace and shopping experience.
- Responsive design ensures smooth usability across all mobile screen sizes while preserving the complete purchasing workflow.
