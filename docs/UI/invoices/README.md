# Purpose

The Invoice module manages all official billing documents generated by the **Shop** / **FreshFlow** platform.

An invoice represents the legal financial record of a completed business transaction. It details line-item prices, GST tax breakdown (CGST, SGST, IGST), shipping fees, customer billing details, and payment transaction IDs.

---

# Goals

The Invoice module aims to:

* Generate tax-compliant invoices for completed orders.
* Maintain permanent, immutable billing records.
* Support sequential invoice numbering (`INV-YYYY-XXXX`).
* Provide browser-printable invoice formats.
* Calculate state-based GST rates accurately based on `gst_configurations`.
* Support administrative filtering by status (Paid, Issued, Cancelled).

# Module Boundaries

The Invoice module **does** manage:

* Invoice records.
* Invoice numbers.
* Billing snapshots.
* Invoice totals.
* Customer billing information.
* Invoice status.
* Printable invoices.

The Invoice module **does not** manage:

* Products
* Categories
* Inventory
* Warehouse
* Orders
* Payments
* Reports

It consumes data from these modules but never owns them.

---

# Invoice Philosophy

Invoices are official business documents.

Once finalized, invoices should never lose historical accuracy.

Even if:

* Product prices change.
* Product names change.
* Customer profile changes.
* Company information changes.

Historical invoices should remain exactly as they were when generated.

FreshFlow achieves this by storing invoice snapshots.

---

# Invoice Lifecycle

The Invoice module follows this lifecycle:

```text
Order Completed
        ↓
Invoice Generated
        ↓
Invoice Saved
        ↓
Invoice Viewed
        ↓
Invoice Printed
        ↓
Invoice Archived
```

Invoices are permanent business records.

---

# Invoice Number

Every invoice must have a unique invoice number.

Example:

```text
INV-000001
INV-000002
INV-000003
```

Invoice numbers must:

* Be unique.
* Never be reused.
* Never change.
* Be searchable.

# Relationship with Orders

Invoices are created from completed orders.

Relationship:

```text
Order
      ↓
Invoice
```

An invoice references an order but remains independent after creation.

Changes to an order must never modify an existing invoice.

---

# Relationship with Products

Historical invoices always display the original billing information.

# Security

Invoices contain business financial information.

Access must be restricted.

Business Owner:

Can:

* View invoices.
* Search invoices.
* Print invoices.

Buyer:

Can:

* View their own invoices (Future).

Unauthorized users must never access invoice information.

---

# Business Rules

The Invoice module follows these rules:

* Every invoice belongs to one company.
* Every invoice belongs to one completed order.
* Every invoice has one unique invoice number.
* Every invoice contains at least one line item.
* Invoice snapshots are immutable.
* Historical invoices never change.
* Invoice totals must remain accurate.
* Invoice records are never permanently deleted.
